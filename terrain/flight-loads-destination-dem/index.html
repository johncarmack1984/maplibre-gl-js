flyTo right after terrain is switched on

Test pages for #8732, the fix for the flight reported on #8590. The map sits at sea level at 10.5 E, terrain is switched on, and flyTo starts at once toward a 560 m slope at 15 E, zoom 17, pitch 60. Every DEM tile arrives the given delay after it is requested. Each link runs upstream main (6d26435e5) or the fix branch john/flight-loads-destination-dem (34f5bc724), and the panel reports the lowest camera clearance above the ground and the worst center elevation error during the flight. On the branch, the flight loads the DEM under its destination when it starts, and the center elevation moves with the center along the path, so the camera no longer climbs onto the slope as it zooms in at the end.
